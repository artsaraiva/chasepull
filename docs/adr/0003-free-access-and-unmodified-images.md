# Access stays free and card images are shown unmodified

Two external terms constrain the product, and neither is visible in the code.

The Wizards of the Coast Fan Content Policy forbids requiring payment, subscriptions or registration to access fan content, and forbids selling it. Scryfall's API terms forbid paywalling its data. So ChasePull has no paywall, no premium tier and no required account. Ads, sponsorships and donations remain allowed later, as long as they don't block access. Every page shows the Fan Content notice and credits Scryfall and MTGJSON.

Scryfall's terms also forbid cropping, distorting, color-shifting or watermarking card images. Images are therefore hotlinked from Scryfall's CDN exactly as provided. They are not routed through image optimization or re-encoding.
